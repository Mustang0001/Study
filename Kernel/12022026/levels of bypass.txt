# Step 0 — Understand Raspberry Pi 4 Reality

RPi 4 Ethernet controller:

Broadcom BCM54213 PHY
Broadcom GENET MAC
Connected internally over SoC interconnect (not PCIe NIC)

This is NOT:

* Intel ixgbe
* Mellanox ConnectX
* PCIe polling-mode NIC

So DPDK full bypass is not realistically supported.

That does NOT mean you cannot bypass.

It means you must choose the correct layer.
